# Hareesh Nuthi

### Analyst, builder, and careful observer

I am interested in the moment a complicated system becomes legible: a risk hidden in a pattern, a household decision that needs a clearer model, or a small everyday ritual that deserves better tools.

My work sits at the intersection of **data analytics**, **internal audit and risk**, and **applied machine learning**. I enjoy moving from an untidy real-world question to a considered product: understanding the domain, modelling it carefully, testing the edge cases, and making the final experience feel calm and useful.

## How I work

I tend to build with both rigour and curiosity.

- I care about **evidence and traceability**: rules should be explainable, figures should reconcile, and uncertainty should be visible rather than hidden.
- I care about **the human surface of a tool**: a model can be technically sound and still fail if it is confusing, intrusive, or indifferent to the person using it.
- I am drawn to **privacy-respecting, resilient software**: local-first data where appropriate, clear boundaries around sensitive information, thoughtful error states, and documentation that lets a project be understood long after it was first built.
- I keep learning through making—especially with Python, TypeScript, data analysis, machine learning, and practical AI systems.

Some of my most substantial work is deliberately private: analytical tools, family learning products, and personal decision-support systems. The public projects below are a small, shareable window into that practice.
